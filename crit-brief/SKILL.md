name: crit-brief
description: Crit a creative project brief or plan — scope, audience, deliverables, constraints. Use when a designer says "crit my brief", "stress-test this project", or shares a plan for a graphic, motion, or visual art job.
---

Read [`../crit-shared/METHOD.md`](../crit-shared/METHOD.md) and run it on the user's **brief**: a plan to make something.

The tree's root branches:

- **Goal**: what does success look like, and who judges it?
- **Audience**: who, where, in what state of mind?
- **Deliverables**: every asset, size, format, and platform — listed exhaustively.
- **Constraints**: budget, deadline, brand rules, technical limits, approvals.
- **Direction**: references, tone, what's off-limits.
- **Tools and process**: the tool question, plus who does what and when feedback happens.

Open on direction and approach; narrow hard on deliverables and constraints — that's where vague briefs cost the most later.

Done when every deliverable is listed with its format and every constraint is named, with other branches settled or parked. Next command: `/crit-prompt` if AI tools are in the process, otherwise `/crit-work` once there's something to look at.
