---
name: crit-concept
description: Crit a raw creative idea until it becomes a clear vision. Use when a designer or artist says "crit my concept", "poke holes in my idea", or shares an unmade graphic, motion, or visual art idea.
---

Read [`../crit-shared/METHOD.md`](../crit-shared/METHOD.md) and run it on the user's **concept**: an idea that hasn't been made yet.

The tree's root branches, in rough order:

- **The core**: what is this, in one sentence? What's the feeling or the provocation underneath it?
- **Why this**: what makes it worth making, and what makes it _yours_?
- **Audience and context**: who meets it, where, and for how long?
- **Form**: discipline, format, medium, scale — and the tool question once form is clear.
- **Direction**: the look, the references, and what it must never become.

Open hardest here: the concept stage is where alternatives are cheapest. Every root branch gets its "take it further" and "other versions" moves before narrowing.

Done when the user can state the concept, its audience, its form, and its direction, with every other branch settled or parked. Next command: `/crit-brief`.
